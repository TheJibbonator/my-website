Our Story

Founded in 2018 by two passionate Italian immigrants, Dominic and Matteo, Dominic's was born out of a desire to bring genuine Naples-style pizza to Medina, Ohio.

We believe that legendary pizza starts with incredible ingredients. That's why our dough is freshly prepared every morning by our friends at the Medina Artisan Bakery.

What Makes Us Special

  • Free High-Speed Wi-Fi: Great for students, remote work, or hanging out with friends.
  • Family-Size Seating: Spacious indoor booths and cozy outdoor seating for group gatherings.
  • Vegan & Dietary Friendly: Vegan mozzarella, gluten-free crusts, and dairy-free options available.
  • Military & Student Discount: 10% off all orders for active military, veterans, and students.
